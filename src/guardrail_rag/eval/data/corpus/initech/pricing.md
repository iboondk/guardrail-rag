# Initech Pricing

Ledger Suite is licensed per company with a base fee of 800 dollars per month, which covers up to 25 users. Each additional user costs 20 dollars per month.

Staff Scheduler is priced at 4 dollars per employee per month, with a minimum of 40 employees. Payroll Bridge charges 6 dollars per employee per pay run plus a one-time setup fee of 1,500 dollars.

Annual contracts receive a 10 percent discount on all modules. Multi-year contracts of three years or more receive a further negotiated discount that is set out in the order form.

Support is included in the base fee for the first year. Premium support, which provides a named engineer and a two-hour response target, costs 300 dollars per month.

Discounts for public sector customers are listed in a separate schedule. Each schedule states the approval level required, the documents needed, and the timeline for a decision.
