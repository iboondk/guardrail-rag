# Initech Offsite Plan 2026

The annual offsite takes place over three days in late September at a lakeside venue. The agenda covers the product strategy for next year, budget planning and team reviews.

Day one is reserved for product teams. They present roadmaps for Ledger Suite, Staff Scheduler and Payroll Bridge, and they agree on the top three bets for each product.

Day two focuses on the company-wide budget. Finance will walk through the cost forecast, and each department head will propose changes that are voted on by the leadership group.

Day three is for team-building and open discussion. Attendees must complete a short feedback form before they leave, and the results are shared with the leadership group within two weeks.

Travel for the offsite is booked through the central travel desk. Staff who need accessibility support should tell the people team at least three weeks before the event so that the venue can prepare.
