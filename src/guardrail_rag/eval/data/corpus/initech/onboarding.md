# Initech Onboarding

Ledger Suite onboarding starts with a data mapping workshop. Initech consultants review the chart of accounts, the tax codes and the approval chains, which usually takes two days on site or over video.

Staff Scheduler setup is self-service. Managers add shift patterns, then invite staff by email, and the first schedule can be published within an afternoon.

Payroll Bridge requires a parallel run for at least one pay cycle. During the parallel run, the system computes pay without sending money, and the finance team compares totals with the existing payroll provider.

Every new customer receives a named success manager for the first 90 days. The success manager runs a fortnightly check-in and tracks open items in the shared project board.

Customers may choose a weekend workshop instead of a weekday one. Weekend sessions are limited to eight participants and must be booked at least ten days in advance through the success manager.
