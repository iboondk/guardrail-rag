# Initech Systems Refund Policy

Initech Systems accepts refund requests for Ledger Suite licenses within 45 days of purchase. Requests made after that date are handled as credit toward a future renewal, not as cash.

Staff Scheduler refunds follow a shorter window of 21 days. Any hours already scheduled and billed to clients count as consumed and cannot be reversed.

Payroll Bridge subscriptions are refunded pro rata for each full month remaining, but the one-time setup fee is never refunded. Setup fees cover the bank file testing that Initech performs before the first run.

Refunds are paid by bank transfer within 15 business days after approval. Customers outside the United States may need to supply a tax form before the transfer is released.

Refund requests must include the original invoice number and the reason for the request. Requests without an invoice number are returned to the sender and cannot be processed until the missing details are provided.
