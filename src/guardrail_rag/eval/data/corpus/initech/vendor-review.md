# Initech Vendor Review Process

Every new vendor must pass a risk review before the first purchase order is raised. The procurement team checks financial stability, insurance cover and data handling practices.

Vendors that will access customer data are assessed every year. Reviews cover encryption practices, subprocessor lists and incident history over the previous 24 months.

Scores run from one to five. A vendor that scores below three cannot be approved without a written exception from the chief operating officer. Exceptions expire after twelve months.

Contract renewals trigger a short review that focuses on price changes and service levels. The renewal review must finish at least 30 days before the current term ends.

Vendors that provide physical equipment are reviewed on delivery reliability and warranty handling as well. The receiving team records any defects in the vendor scorecard, and repeated defects can trigger an early review.

Results of each review are stored in the procurement register, which the internal audit team checks once a year for completeness and accuracy.
