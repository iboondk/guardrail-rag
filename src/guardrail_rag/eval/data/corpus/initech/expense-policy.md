# Initech Expense Policy

Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted later require approval from the chief financial officer and are paid in the next quarterly cycle.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day within the continental United States, and up to 85 dollars per day abroad. Receipts are required for any single item above 25 dollars.

Mileage is reimbursed at 0.58 dollars per mile for personal vehicles used on approved trips. Parking and tolls are reimbursed in full with a receipt.

Hotel stays must be booked through the approved travel portal. Stays above 220 dollars per night need pre-approval from the department head.

Personal expenses paid by mistake with a company card must be repaid within 14 days. Repeated mistakes are reviewed by the finance lead, who may suspend card access for the affected employee until the review is complete.
