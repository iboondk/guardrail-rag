# Acme Onboarding Guide

New workspaces start with a 14-day trial that includes all Pro features. Administrators invite teammates from the Members page, and each invite expires after 72 hours.

The first integration should be the source database connector. Acme Cloud Labs recommends connecting a read-only replica rather than the primary database, because the sync job reads every table once an hour.

Within the first week, admins should set up single sign-on with their identity provider. SAML and OIDC are both supported on the Pro plan. Customers on the Business tier can also join a shared onboarding channel with their support engineer.

For questions during setup, write to onboarding@example.com. The onboarding team replies on weekdays between 9 and 17 Central European Time. Customers who finish the checklist in the dashboard receive a free workshop credit worth two hours of consulting.

Teams that finish onboarding within the first two weeks report higher adoption in later months. The customer success team tracks this milestone and sends a short summary to each workspace admin once the checklist is complete.
