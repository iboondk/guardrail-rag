# Acme Cloud Labs Refund Policy

Acme Cloud Labs offers a full refund for monthly subscriptions that are cancelled within 30 days of the first charge. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and usually appear within 7 business days.

Annual plans follow a different rule. Customers who cancel an annual plan within 60 days receive a prorated refund for the unused months, minus a 10 percent administration fee. After 60 days, annual plans are not refundable, but the plan stays active until the end of its term.

Refunds are not available for usage-based charges that have already been consumed, such as API calls or stored gigabytes. Credits issued as goodwill gestures do not convert to cash.

To request a refund, open a ticket from the billing page and select the reason. Refund requests are reviewed by the billing team, and the team answers within two business days. Customers in regions with mandatory cooling-off periods receive the longer period required by local law.
