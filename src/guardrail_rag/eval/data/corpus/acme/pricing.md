# Acme Pricing

The Starter plan costs 19 US dollars per seat per month and includes 50 gigabytes of storage. The Pro plan costs 49 dollars per seat per month and includes 500 gigabytes of storage plus single sign-on.

The Business plan is priced at 89 dollars per seat per month. It adds audit logs kept for one year, a dedicated Slack channel, and priority support with a four hour response target.

Annual billing gives a discount of 15 percent on every plan. Discounts do not stack with promotional credits. Enterprise pricing is negotiated and starts at 25,000 dollars per year.

Additional storage can be purchased in blocks of 100 gigabytes for 12 dollars per month. API requests above the monthly quota are billed at 0.002 dollars per call.

Nonprofit organizations can request a separate price list after verification of their status. The verification takes about three business days, and approved organizations receive the Business plan features at a reduced seat price.
