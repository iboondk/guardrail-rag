# Acme Product Roadmap 2026

The first half of 2026 focuses on the Insights module. Beta access opens in February for Business customers, and general availability is planned for June. Insights adds cohort charts and scheduled email digests.

In the second half, the team will ship the managed vector search feature. It stores embeddings beside the source tables and keeps tenant data separate with per-workspace keys.

The mobile dashboard is planned for the fourth quarter. It will show alerts and daily usage, but editing of reports will stay on the web app for now.

Items that are not on this roadmap include a self-hosted edition and a marketplace for third-party connectors. These may be reconsidered after the Insights launch, based on feedback from the beta program.

Dates on this roadmap are estimates and may move. Customers with a signed Business agreement receive a notice thirty days before any change to a committed release date, together with the reason for the change.
