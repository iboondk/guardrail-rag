# Globex Pricing

Freight Hub is billed per shipment. The Standard rate is 2.40 dollars per shipment for domestic lanes and 4.10 dollars for cross-border lanes. Volume customers above 10,000 shipments per month receive a 12 percent discount.

Route Planner costs 130 dollars per vehicle per year. A fleet of 50 vehicles therefore costs 6,500 dollars annually before any volume discount.

Customs Desk charges 35 dollars per declaration, with a minimum monthly commitment of 20 declarations. Declarations filed above the commitment are billed at 28 dollars each.

Payment terms are net 30 for approved accounts. Late payments accrue interest at 1 percent per month, and accounts more than 60 days overdue are suspended.

Surcharges apply to shipments that need temperature control. The surcharge is 0.75 dollars per shipment and is shown separately on each invoice, so customers can see it before the shipment is booked.

Quotes for custom lanes are valid for 30 days from the date they are issued, and any change in fuel surcharge is passed through at cost.
