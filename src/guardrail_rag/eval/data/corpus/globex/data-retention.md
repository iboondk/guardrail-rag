# Globex Data Retention

Shipment records are kept for seven years to meet customs audit requirements. After that period they are anonymized and aggregated, and the personal details of drivers and consignees are removed.

GPS traces from vehicles are retained for 90 days. Route Planner keeps the planning inputs for the length of the subscription and deletes them 30 days after termination.

Support chat transcripts are kept for 18 months. Customers can request earlier deletion, which Globex completes within 30 days unless a legal hold applies.

Backups rotate every 14 days and are stored in a second region. Deleted records are removed from backups at the next rotation.

Records placed under a legal hold are excluded from the normal deletion schedule. The compliance owner reviews each hold every six months and releases it once the matter is closed. Holds are logged, and the customer is told when a hold affects their data.
