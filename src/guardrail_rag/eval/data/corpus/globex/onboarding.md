# Globex Onboarding

Every new Freight Hub account is assigned a dedicated onboarding manager for the first 45 days. The manager schedules a kickoff call, imports the carrier list, and confirms the default warehouse addresses.

Route Planner onboarding takes about three working sessions. The first session maps depots, the second loads historical delivery data, and the third tunes driver shift rules. Customers usually reach their first optimized route on day nine.

Customs Desk requires a filed power of attorney before the first declaration. The broker uploads the scanned form to the compliance vault, and Globex verifies it within two business days.

Training is included for up to 20 users. Additional seats can be trained through the online academy, which offers twelve modules with completion certificates.

Customers who want a custom integration can book a solution review in the first two weeks. The review covers carrier onboarding, custom fields and reporting exports, and it produces a written plan with estimated effort.
