# Globex Service Level Agreement

Freight Hub guarantees 99.5 percent monthly uptime measured at the API gateway. Scheduled maintenance windows are excluded from the calculation when they are announced at least seven days in advance.

If uptime falls below the guarantee, the customer receives a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly fee for each full percent below target, up to a maximum of 30 percent of that month's fee.

Route Planner offers a lower guarantee of 99.0 percent because its optimization jobs run in batches. Batch delays longer than 20 minutes count as downtime for the affected depot.

Customers must claim credits within 30 days of the affected month. Globex reviews claims against its own monitoring logs and replies within five business days.

Incidents that last longer than four hours trigger a written post-incident report. The report is sent to the account owner within ten business days and includes root cause, customer impact and prevention steps.
