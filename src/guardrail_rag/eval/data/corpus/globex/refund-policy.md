# Globex Logistics Refund Policy

Globex Logistics refunds freight bookings that are cancelled at least 72 hours before the scheduled pickup. Cancellations inside that window keep 50 percent of the booking fee. Refunds go back to the card that paid the booking.

Route Planner subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Annual Route Planner licenses are not refundable after 14 days, but unused seats can be moved to another team at no cost.

Customs brokerage fees are non-refundable once a declaration has been filed with the authority. Duties and tariffs paid to a government are passed through and cannot be recovered by Globex.

Refund requests go through the Freight Hub support queue. Globex processes approved refunds within 10 business days. Disputed amounts are held until both parties agree on a figure.

Refunds for add-on services follow the same timing as the base service they are attached to. Credit notes are issued when a refund is not possible, and they can be applied to any future invoice.
